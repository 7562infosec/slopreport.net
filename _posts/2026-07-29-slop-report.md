### 21. [AI-Found Bugs Aren't Proving Any Easier to Exploit Despite the Hype](https://it.slashdot.org/story/26/07/28/212240/ai-found-bugs-arent-proving-any-easier-to-exploit-despite-the-hype?utm_source=rss1.0mainlinkanon&utm_medium=feed)
*Slashdot*  - · Jul 28

A recent analysis by VulnCheck reveals that AI-assisted vulnerability discovery has not
significantly increased the exploitation of previously identified bugs, with only 1.3% of AI-found
vulnerabilities being exploited, similar to traditional methods. This finding casts doubt on the
narrative that AI, such as Anthropic's Project Glasswing, is creating weaponizable vulnerabilities
at an alarming rate, instead suggesting that AI is useful for uncovering flaws that can be patched
before malicious actors exploit them. Patrick Garrity from VulnCheck emphasizes that while the risks
associated with AI in cybersecurity are real, the current impact is modest and may have been
overstated.
